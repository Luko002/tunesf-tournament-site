Privacy policy

How the Tunisian Esports Federation (“TUNESF”, “we”, “us”) handles personal data when you use this competition and player platform.

At a glance

Competition records and player/team profile details may be visible to the public. FACEIT and game-ID links are optional. We do not sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising.

01

Who is responsible for your data?

The platform is operated by the Tunisian Esports Federation (TUNESF). For privacy questions or requests, contact us at contact@tunesf.com or write to 5 Rue Hédi Khfécha, El Menzah 1, Tunis, Tunisia.

For this platform, TUNESF decides why account, profile, and competition records are used. Some data is handled by service providers on our behalf, as described below.

02

What data do we handle?

  • Account details: email address, public username, password authentication handled by Supabase, and any optional Discord username you add.
  • Player and competition details: game, region, team and roster membership, tournament registrations, match schedules, lineups, results, rankings, awards, reports, and in-platform messages or notifications.
  • Images and evidence: profile avatars, team logos, tournament cover images, and match evidence uploaded to the platform.
  • Optional game connections: for Rocket League, Mobile Legends: Bang Bang, EA SPORTS FC, and eFootball, the game ID, optional platform/server, proof link, review status, and review note you submit. These links are reviewed by authorized TUNESF administrators and do not automatically sync game statistics.
  • Optional FACEIT connection: when you connect or refresh FACEIT, we receive your FACEIT player identifier, nickname, avatar and, when refreshed, profile details and public CS2 statistics such as level, ELO, region, and lifetime stats. FACEIT sign-in requests the OpenID identity scope; the access token is used to complete the lookup and is not stored as your TUNESF profile data.
  • Technical and security information: basic request, device, and diagnostic information processed by our hosting, database, and authentication providers, plus security and administrative audit records.

Please do not put sensitive personal information, passwords, or government identity documents in a game-ID proof link, public profile, image, chat, or match-evidence upload.

03

Public profiles and private information

Your email, password, private notifications, and game-ID proof submissions are not part of your public profile. Authorized TUNESF administrators can access information needed to operate competitions, review identity submissions, investigate abuse, or provide support.

Depending on the feature, public pages may display your username, avatar, team and roster, game, region, tournament participation, match results, and awards. If you connect FACEIT, your FACEIT profile is set to visible by default; you can turn that visibility off in your player dashboard. Public information can be copied or indexed by others, so only publish details you are comfortable sharing.

Avatars, team logos, and other materials placed in public areas may be viewable by anyone. Match evidence and tournament records may be available to tournament officials or other authorized users under the relevant competition workflow.

04

Why do we use it?

We use data to create and secure accounts; provide player, team, tournament, match, and federation tools; verify submitted game identities; display competition results and public profiles; send service and competition notifications; handle reports, appeals, and support; prevent fraud, cheating, impersonation, and abuse; and meet applicable legal obligations.

Where the EU/UK GDPR applies, the relevant lawful basis will generally be providing the service you request or administering an event you join, our legitimate interests in running fair and secure competitions, your consent for optional connections or visibility choices, or compliance with a legal obligation. You can withdraw consent for an optional connection by disconnecting it or changing its visibility, without affecting processing already carried out.

05

Who receives your data?

We disclose data only as needed to operate the platform:

  • Supabase provides authentication, the database, file storage, and server functions. The project database is hosted in the EU West 1 region (Ireland). See the Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel hosts and delivers this website through its network. Its infrastructure may process technical request data in more than one location. See the Vercel Privacy Notice.
  • FACEIT receives information necessary to authorize the optional FACEIT connection and return the identity/profile or CS2 data you requested. FACEIT separately handles that information under its Privacy Policy.
  • Web asset providers may receive your browser’s IP address and request details when it loads externally hosted Google Fonts or icon-library files (unpkg/jsDelivr fallback). See Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Other users and event officials see data that the service makes public or that they need for an event, roster, match, report, or administrative task.

We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect users or the integrity of a competition, or in connection with a lawful reorganization of the service. We do not sell personal data.

06

International transfers

TUNESF is based in Tunisia, while the project database is hosted in Ireland and some website, identity-provider, or content-delivery requests may be handled elsewhere. This means information can be processed outside the country where you live.

International transfers are subject to applicable safeguards and legal requirements. Tunisia’s Organic Law No. 2004-63 addresses adequate protection and authorization for transfers abroad; where the GDPR applies, its international-transfer rules also apply. The privacy notice does not itself grant a regulatory authorization. Contact TUNESF if you need information about the safeguards or authorization relevant to a particular transfer. You can read the INPDP’s data-protection texts and the official GDPR text.

07

Retention and security

We keep account and connection information while your account or connection is active and for as long as needed to run the relevant competition, resolve appeals or disputes, protect platform integrity, and meet legal obligations. Official match, roster, and tournament outcomes may be retained after an account closes so the competition record remains reliable. Provider logs and backups may follow the provider’s own retention schedules. Contact us for information about a specific record.

We use access controls and technical safeguards designed to protect information. No online service can promise absolute security; use a unique password and do not share your sign-in details.

08

Your choices and privacy rights

You can update profile details in your account, control FACEIT profile visibility, and manage optional connections from the player dashboard. You can ask us to access, correct, update, or delete personal data by emailing contact@tunesf.com. We may ask for reasonable information to verify the request. Some competition or security records may need to be retained where the law allows or requires it.

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may also have rights to object, restrict processing, receive a portable copy, withdraw consent, or complain to a data-protection authority. In Tunisia, you may contact the National Authority for the Protection of Personal Data (INPDP). If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, you may contact your local supervisory authority.

09

Young players

Young people may participate only where permitted by applicable law and the rules of the relevant event. If you are under the age at which you can agree to these terms in your country, ask a parent or legal guardian to review and approve your use. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal data inappropriately can contact us.

10

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the service or applicable rules change. We will update the effective date and provide a notice on the site for material changes.
